# STEP 48G — read-only Metrics continuity

The verifier independently reconstructs attempt/result identities, exact source
row selection, normalized payload, scope, sequential chronology, raw commitments,
receipt identity and availability binding. It never writes, repairs, captures or
opens a scientific ledger. Config is authenticated using the frozen activation.

SUCCESS requires the entire chain. Leftover receipt/available artifacts from a
FAILED attempt never count. Missing result is INCOMPLETE; malformed or mismatched
artifacts are INVALID. A closed slot without attempt is derived MISSED_SLOT;
no attempt/result is fabricated. Open absent slots are NOT_DUE.

The certificate counts closed slots from activation only. Current/longest streaks
require consecutive independently verified SUCCESS. 81 current successes mean
DATA_PLANE_READINESS_ONLY, never research qualification, Regime, Mechanics or Edge.
INVALID is a read-only classification: no tool here can repair or promote it.

Hashes detect inconsistent content; they are not signatures proving Binance origin
against an adversary able to rewrite every file and its commitments. The trusted
capture boundary and protected local storage remain required. Concurrent capture
may yield transient INCOMPLETE; a certificate is only about its declared as-of
time and the artifacts read. No runtime/heartbeat evidence is substituted.
