# ADR-OMA-V2-005 — Longitudinal Edge Stability

## Status
Accepted as research governance; no trading promotion.

## Decision
A conditional effect is not an Edge merely because it backtests profitably. OMA-CORE must preserve period, venue, instrument, sample size, effect magnitude and uncertainty, and evaluate longitudinal stability before Knowledge/Criterion promotion.

## Required gates
1. Causal feature construction from information available at decision time.
2. Minimum sample gate.
3. Sign/effect stability across independent periods.
4. Moving-block bootstrap because overlapping forward returns are dependent.
5. Cross-venue/instrument evidence kept distinct.
6. Execution translation with next-open entry and explicit costs.
7. Prospective untouched validation.
8. Only then Knowledge/Criterion candidacy.

## 2025→2026 finding
Static Regime v1 has weak transportability. Context, participation and transition descriptors are research features, not validated trading filters. No tested contextual extension currently clears promotion.

## 2024
Raw 2024 bytes must be reintroduced and recomputed under the frozen protocol. Historical summaries are evidence, not a substitute for reproducible raw-data evaluation.
