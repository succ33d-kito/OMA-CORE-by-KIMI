# ADR-OMA-V2-008 — Calendar inference and prospective hypothesis

Status: accepted as research infrastructure; no evidence or trading promotion.

The 2024→2025 per-cell bootstrap selected observations after filtering, which can break calendar dependence. `calendar_block_inference.py` now samples contiguous 168-hour blocks from the full UTC calendar, jointly across the five forward horizons. Missing hours remain absent. The maximum standardized deviation gives simultaneous 95% intervals for the predeclared family. The implementation tests deterministic resampling, missing hours, duplicate decisions, overlapping arms and minimum N. It does not retroactively convert earlier exploratory intervals into confirmatory evidence.

One future hypothesis is frozen in `research/metrics_2024_2025/PROSPECTIVE_H1_PREREGISTRATION.json`, protected by a SHA-256 guard. It compares OI-rise-plus-taker-buy hours with other eligible hours *within Regime range*, using 4h as primary. Both arms share venue and horizon. The feed must log `available_at` for each bar and metric. A decision occurs five minutes after the bar closes only if both inputs arrived by then; entry is the next full UTC hour open after that decision, followed by the specified number of full hourly bars. Late observations are excluded rather than backdated. The other horizons form one jointly controlled family; none can rescue a failed primary. Minimum 90 calendar days and 200 observations per arm per horizon, 2,000 resamples, seed 1729, and a fixed 10-basis-point round-trip research cost threshold are set before future data. The cost is a hurdle, not measured execution evidence.

A narrower trend-up candidate was rejected solely on historical exposure counts (37 and 31 target observations in 2024 and 2025); no new conditional return audit was run to choose the replacement. Historical 2024–2025 outcomes remain contaminated by prior exploration and cannot confirm this hypothesis. The prospective evaluator rejects pre-2026-10-01 decisions, gaps, duplicate bars, late bars or metrics and changed protocol bytes. The original archive timestamps cannot stand in for prospective receipt timestamps. It emits research results only; prospective success would still require independent replication, realized costs, risk and the other Criterion gates in ADR-OMA-V2-005.
